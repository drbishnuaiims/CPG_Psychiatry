The story

A clinician with a builder's operating system.

CURRENT FOCUS

Clinical credibility + digital intelligence

Understanding people clinically. Understanding systems entrepreneurially. Applying both to modern healthcare.

I am a psychiatrist by training and an entrepreneur by temperament. My journey has crossed technology, hospitality, consumer products, community initiatives and healthcare.

That combination created a perspective I now bring to psychiatry: the best digital presence is not about becoming an influencer. It is about making authentic expertise easier to find, understand and trust.

My current mission is to help psychiatrists across the world build a professional digital front door — one that reflects the quality of their clinical work and supports the growth of a sustainable practice.
