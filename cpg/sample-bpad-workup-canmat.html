10. Pharmacological Treatment Plan — Sample

CANMAT/ISBD 2018: first-line acute mania options include lithium, quetiapine, divalproex, asenapine, aripiprazole, paliperidone, risperidone and cariprazine. First-line combinations include quetiapine, aripiprazole, risperidone or asenapine with lithium/divalproex; combination treatment is particularly relevant when index severity is high.
ComponentSample planRationale/monitoring
Antimanic agentLithium — initiate/titrate according to current local protocol and patient factors.First-line acute mania option. Acute trough target in source: 0.8–1.2 mEq/L; obtain approximately 5 days after dose titration, at trough about 12 h after last dose.
AntipsychoticQuetiapine — initiate/titrate according to current product information/local protocol.First-line acute mania option; appropriate to consider when psychosis is prominent. Monitor sedation, orthostasis, weight and metabolic parameters.
Acute agitationUse an appropriate short-term protocol based on severity, route and local emergency policy.CANMAT 2018 lists several rapid-acting/IM options; current local protocol and product labeling take precedence.
AntidepressantNot initiated during this acute manic episode.Establish mood stability first; assess switch risk and indication carefully.

Why lithium + quetiapine in this sample?

  • High severity: psychosis and marked functional impairment support consideration of combination treatment.
  • Evidence: both are first-line acute mania agents; quetiapine + lithium is a first-line combination in the source.
  • Longitudinal value: lithium and quetiapine are also first-line maintenance options.
  • Individualisation: final selection must incorporate prior response, tolerability, comorbidity, substance use, adherence and patient/caregiver preference.

13. Longitudinal Maintenance Plan

QuestionPlan
What worked acutely?Where possible, continue an effective acute agent into maintenance.
Predominantly manic recurrence?Give weight to prior response to effective antimanic treatment.
Predominantly depressive recurrence?Consider lithium, quetiapine and lamotrigine among relevant first-line options.
Mixed features?Reassess antidepressant exposure and choose an appropriate switch-risk profile.
Rapid cycling?Assess thyroid disease, antidepressants, stimulants and substances.
Antipsychotic + lithium/divalproex used acutely?2018 source notes evidence for continuing the atypical antipsychotic during the first 6 months after response, followed by risk-benefit reassessment.

First-line maintenance options in the source include lithium, quetiapine, divalproex, lamotrigine, asenapine and aripiprazole; first-line combinations include quetiapine + lithium/divalproex and aripiprazole + lithium/divalproex.
